Waiting on you

Not worth your money

If someone approaches offering SEO services, this is the list to push back with. Everything here was genuinely considered and rejected for your specific situation.

  • A page for every town. Six near-identical "Gardener in [Town]" pages, with no reviews or local photos to make them different, is the exact pattern Google discounts. Worth revisiting once you've got reviews and jobs to show in each place, not now.
  • A blog. Weekly gardening articles are a poor use of a one-man band's evenings. Reviews will out-earn them many times over.
  • Star ratings in the site's code before you have real reviews. Google treats invented ratings as spam and the penalty is severe.
  • "AI SEO" extras like an llms.txt file. Built for large sites with hundreds of pages. Nothing to gain here.
  • Yelp, Angi, Houzz, Porch, BBB. Either American, or no longer meaningful for UK trades.
  • Backlink packages. Nothing about your situation is solved by bought links, and they carry real risk.

What to expect, honestly

No one can make a two-day-old website rank quickly, and anyone promising otherwise is selling something. A realistic path:

  • Next week or two: your site gets indexed and starts showing up when someone searches your business name. The dead address drops away.
  • Next month or two: with reviews coming in steadily, you start appearing in the map results for searches near Lancing and Shoreham.
  • Longer: the further towns, Findon and Ferring, take longest. How close you are to the person searching is one of the biggest factors in local results, and nothing on a website overrides that.

The honest summary: your website is not what's holding you back. Time and reviews are, and only one of those is in your control.
